Procedural wood no longer repeats on long or wide surfaces. Planks now vary in width, shade, taper and where the pith sits, and the pith drifts along each plank. Before, every plank was 180 mm wide and repeated the same arch about every half metre. Pores are now thin, sparse dashes that settle to an even tone when smaller than a pixel. Before, they were dark blotches that hid the grain on edges and on faces seen head-on.
